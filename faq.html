Dog Training FAQs – Elite K9 Group

Find answers to frequently asked questions about professional dog training in Los Angeles, including training costs, payment plans, program duration, obedience training, board and train programs, behavioral modification, and service areas.

Pricing and Payment

How much does professional dog training cost in Los Angeles?

Professional dog training at Elite K9 Group in Los Angeles starts at $2,500. The final cost depends on the selected training program, your dog's behavioral needs, training goals, and the duration of the program. We offer a range of professional dog training services, including obedience training, board and train programs, private dog training, and behavioral modification. Schedule a free dog training consultation to receive personalized recommendations and pricing based on your dog's individual needs.

Do you offer payment plans for dog training?

Yes! Elite K9 Group offers flexible payment plans for dog training programs. Since training spots are limited, a deposit is required to reserve your dog's place in the program. The remaining balance can be paid in installments throughout the training period. Our payment plans allow you to spread the cost of professional dog training over the duration of the program. Ask about payment options.

Dog Training Programs

How long does a dog training program take?

The duration of a dog training program depends on your dog's age, temperament, previous training experience, behavioral challenges, and individual training goals. Some of our clients have completed four-week board and train programs, while others have participated in eight-week obedience and off-leash training programs. Dogs with more complex behavioral challenges or specialized training goals may require additional time. During your free consultation, our professional dog trainers can recommend an appropriate training schedule based on your dog's individual needs.

What dog training services do you offer in Los Angeles?

Elite K9 Group offers a wide range of professional dog training services in Sherman Oaks and throughout the greater Los Angeles area, including:

• Dog Obedience Training – Basic, advanced, and off-leash obedience training.
• Board and Train Programs – Intensive professional training in a structured environment.
• Private Dog Training – Personalized training based on your dog's individual needs.
• Puppy Training – Foundational obedience, socialization, and confidence building.
• Dog Behavior Modification – Training designed to address aggression, reactivity, fear, and other behavioral challenges.
• Service Dog Training – Specialized training to assist individuals with specific needs.
• Personal and Family Protection Dog Training – Specialized protection training focused on obedience, control, and reliability.

Our professional dog trainers tailor each training program to your dog's temperament, behavioral needs, and your goals as an owner. Explore our dog training services.

What is included in your board and train program?

Our board and train program combines boarding with professional dog training in a structured learning environment. Depending on the selected program and your dog's individual needs, training may focus on foundational and advanced obedience, leash manners, recall, impulse control, confidence building, and appropriate responses to everyday distractions. Board and train in Los Angeles can be an effective option for dog owners looking for intensive training or professional assistance with challenging behaviors. Contact our team to learn more about program inclusions, duration, and pricing.

Do you offer dog obedience training for all ages and breeds?

Elite K9 Group offers professional dog obedience training for dogs at different stages of development, from puppies learning foundational skills to adult dogs requiring advanced obedience or behavioral modification. Our experienced dog trainers have worked with a variety of breeds, including German Shepherds, Belgian Malinois, Dutch Shepherds, and Labradoodles. We consider each dog's age, temperament, training history, and individual needs when developing an appropriate training approach. Learn more about dog obedience training.

Do you offer follow-up support after dog training?

Maintaining your dog's progress after professional training requires consistency, regular practice, and reinforcement of learned behaviors at home. At Elite K9 Group, our training approach emphasizes effective communication and building a stronger relationship between dogs and their owners. Contact our team to learn more about post-training guidance, additional private dog training sessions, and follow-up support options available with your selected program.

Dog Behavior Modification

Can you train aggressive dogs or dogs with a history of biting?

Elite K9 Group offers professional aggressive dog training and dog behavior modification in Los Angeles. Our approach focuses on understanding the underlying causes of aggression, which may include fear, frustration, territorial behavior, previous experiences, and environmental triggers. Dogs with a history of biting require an individual behavioral assessment to determine whether our training programs are appropriate for their needs. Our goal is to reduce behavioral risks, improve communication, and help owners manage their dogs more safely and confidently. Severe cases may require additional veterinary or specialist involvement. Learn more about dog behavior modification.

Can you help with leash reactivity and dog aggression?

Yes! Our reactive dog training addresses behavioral challenges such as barking, lunging, excessive pulling, and intense reactions toward other dogs, unfamiliar people, vehicles, and environmental distractions. Reactivity can result from fear, frustration, overexcitement, or a lack of confidence. Our professional dog trainers assess these behaviors to identify triggers and develop an appropriate training strategy. Our goal is to help your dog develop better focus, impulse control, and more manageable responses, making everyday walks and social interactions safer and more enjoyable.

What other behavioral problems can your dog trainers address?

Elite K9 Group provides professional dog behavior modification services designed to address a variety of behavioral challenges, including:

• Excessive barking
• Leash pulling
• Jumping on people
• Puppy biting and mouthing
• Fear-related behaviors
• Territorial behavior
• Poor recall
• Difficulty following commands
• Lack of focus around distractions
• Overexcitement

Our experienced dog trainers evaluate your dog's individual behavior and recommend a suitable training approach to encourage better habits, confidence, obedience, and communication.
